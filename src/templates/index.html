Ask Reddit

A conversational RAG chatbot that mines Reddit for real opinions.

Ask anything — it searches Reddit in real time, embeds and indexes comments via ChromaDB, and generates grounded answers using Groq LLMs.

Limits

Answers reflect only indexed Reddit threads — opinions, not facts — so coverage can be thin, biased, or stale, and safety filters or slow fetches may get in the way.
